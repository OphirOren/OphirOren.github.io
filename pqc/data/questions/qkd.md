# Domain: Quantum Cryptography and Key Distribution

### Q1. In BB84, why do Alice and Bob compare a subset of bits after sifting?
A. To encrypt the quantum channel
B. To estimate errors and detect eavesdropping
C. To create a certificate
D. To increase the key length
**Answer: B**
**Explanation:** Comparing a sample after sifting estimates the quantum bit error rate and helps detect interference or eavesdropping.

### Q2. Which statement about QKD is most accurate?
A. It is a software drop-in for TLS on every network
B. It always replaces authentication
C. It is the same as PQC
D. It can detect eavesdropping but needs special infrastructure and has deployment limits
**Answer: D**
**Explanation:** QKD relies on quantum-channel properties and dedicated setup; it is not a general PQC replacement and still needs classical authentication.
