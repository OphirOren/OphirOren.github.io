About

This app

PQC Practice

A practice-exam app for people learning post-quantum cryptography (PQC). Use it to enrich your knowledge, drill key concepts, and check how ready you are — whether you are studying PQC in general or preparing for an exam-style assessment such as CQSP.

  • Timed mocks and practice mode for self-check
  • Domain drills across foundations, threats, QKD, PQC algorithms, standards, migration, protocols, and cybersecurity fundamentals
  • Exam-style format available: 50 questions · 60 minutes · pass at 66%
  • Loads local Markdown question and answer banks — no cloud account required
